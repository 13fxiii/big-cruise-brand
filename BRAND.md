BIG CRUISE is a Nigerian-leaning X/Twitter community (not a startup, not a transport company, not a yacht club). Organic online family: cruising, good vibes, real conversations, loyalty, roasts, music, Spaces, jobs, birthdays, mutual support.

- Founder: FX — @13fxiii

Personality (18+): Bold, social, fun, cultural, unapologetic, mature, Nigerian, community-driven. Roast you today. Stand for you tomorrow. Not sexually explicit.

## Master logo

The mark is the Part Alternation Mark, Unicode U+303D, commonly typed as 〽️. It is a lopsided capital M / rollercoaster: two peaks, a dip, a drop to the right. Not stacked chevrons. Not a spark. Not a lightning bolt. Not a yacht.

SVG viewBox: `0 0 64 64`
Stroke: width 9, round caps, round joins, fill none, typically `#F5C400` on `#0B0B0B`
Path:

```
M6 36 C9 22 12 8 21 8 C27.5 8 30 20 32 28 C34 20 36.5 8 43 8 C52 8 56 24 60 54
```

- **Primary / Live mark:** circle r=29, stroke 4, dasharray 155 27, mark scaled 0.58 in the centre. Means the Space is on.
- **Midnight mark (avatar, app icon):** filled circle r=32 fill `#0B0B0B`, mark scaled 0.7 in `#F5C400`.
- **Symbol-only:** the path alone. Chest embroidery, 16px digital / 8mm print minimum.
- **Wordmark:** Barlow Condensed ExtraBold, uppercase, tracking ~0.06em, `BIG CRUISE` plus the vector mark at ~0.88em to the right of E. Never the color emoji as the official file.
- Clear space: height of one peak. Upright only.

Eight concepts were scored. Master is concept 08, the mark. Runners-up: Digital Street, Negative Cut.

## Voice

Nigerian, internet-native, funny, warm, confident, authentic, slightly unfiltered, community-focused. Pidgin when it lands. Do not force slang into every sentence.

Do: The Space is on. Come cruise. / We go talk. You know we still dey your side.
Don't: corporate press, slang pile-ups, gold glow.

## Intentionally rejected

Stacked-chevron spark; yachts, boats, anchors; literal danfo bus as a logo (danfo informs color only); gold/neon/chrome; generic African cliches; former name on lockups; smileys, chat bubbles, waveforms; children's palettes.
