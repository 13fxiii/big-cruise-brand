LOGO GEOMETRY (reproduce exactly)
Unicode U+303D Part Alternation Mark. Lopsided M: two peaks, dip, drop right.
SVG viewBox 0 0 64 64. Stroke 9, round caps/joins, fill none.
Path: M6 36 C9 22 12 8 21 8 C27.5 8 30 20 32 28 C34 20 36.5 8 43 8 C52 8 56 24 60 54
Typically stroke #F5C400 on #0B0B0B. Upright. Never italicize into lightning.
Primary Live mark: circle r=29 stroke 4 dasharray 155 27, mark scaled 0.58 centre.
Midnight/avatar: filled circle r=32 #0B0B0B, mark scaled 0.7 in #F5C400.
Wordmark: Barlow Condensed ExtraBold BIG CRUISE + vector mark. Not the color emoji.
Clear space = one peak. Min 16px / 8mm.

MEANING: community, connection, movement, cruising, conversation, belonging.
Not a yacht, boat, anchor, bus, smile, or chat bubble.

COLORS: Midnight #0B0B0B 75-85%. Yellow #F5C400 15-25%. Bone #F3EFE4. Asphalt #161616.
Rejected: gold, neon, chrome, glow, rainbow.

TYPE: Barlow Condensed ExtraBold, IBM Plex Sans, IBM Plex Mono. All SIL OFL.
Rejected: Danfo Google Font as display.

GRAPHICS: -8deg lanes, 2px yellow frame, live ring 18s spin on ring only, grain, night photos.
MERCH: black blanks, yellow mark. SOCIAL: midnight avatar, midnight header.
VOICE: Nigerian, internet-native, funny, warm. Pidgin when it lands.

REJECTED: spark chevrons, yachts, literal danfo logo, gold, generic Africa cliches,
former name, smileys, waveforms, children's palettes.
